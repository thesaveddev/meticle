# Shared policy control standard

This project uses one policy-control standard for every service model: domiciliary care, live-in care and supported living.

## Review

- Every policy has an owner, version, publication status and review due date.
- Review at least annually and after a material incident, regulatory change or service change.
- Overdue reviews remain visible until completed; they are not silently treated as current.

## Approval

- Draft policies may be edited by authorised organisation managers.
- Publication requires an accountable organisation administrator or delegated approver.
- Changes retain the previous version and record who changed, approved and published them.

## Evidence

- Evidence is attributable to a person or system actor, dated, versioned and linked to the relevant policy or control.
- Evidence must record its source and status; uploaded documents must not be treated as approved solely because they exist.
- Evidence exports must preserve the audit trail and service-model context.

These standards apply equally to domiciliary area operations and supported-living site operations. The evidence itself may differ by service model, but the control quality does not.
