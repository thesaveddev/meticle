Q: What is MeticleCare?
A: MeticleCare (https://meticlecare.com) is care operations software for UK domiciliary and supported living providers. It connects care delivery, scheduling, medication (eMAR), risk, compliance, workforce and reporting in one platform.

Q: Who is MeticleCare for?
A: Domiciliary (home care) and supported living providers across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland — registered managers, care workers, family members and owners.

Q: What does the platform cover?
A: Care planning, daily care notes, medication administration (eMAR with 31-day charts), staff rotas and safe staffing, incidents and safeguarding, training compliance, CQC/CIW/Care Inspectorate/RQIA readiness, evidence packs, family portal, reporting, and a mobile app for carers.

Q: Is MeticleCare compliant with UK data protection?
A: MeticleCare hosts production data in the UK, encrypts data in transit (TLS 1.3), isolates tenants at the database level, and offers MFA and auditable record access. See https://meticlecare.com/security and https://meticlecare.com/privacy.

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: Yes — a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. See https://meticlecare.com/pricing.
