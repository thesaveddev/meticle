# Founder CVs — INNF 8.3(d) scaffolds (incomplete by design)

**Status:** internal scaffolds — **these documents are NOT evidence yet and must
not be submitted anywhere in their current state.** They contain structure,
prompts, and evidence pointers; the biographical content can only come from
Adetoye Adenuga and Opeyemi Olorunfemi themselves.

> **⚠️ Rule for whoever fills these in (and for any agent editing this file
> later): DO NOT FABRICATE.** Do not invent employment history, dates,
> qualifications, or achievements. A single invented line in an endorsement,
> funding, or immigration file is a misrepresentation with legal consequences
> and it poisons the credibility of everything else in the pack. Write "not yet
> provided" instead. The assessors of this file will accept a short, true CV;
> nobody accepts a polished false one.

**Why CVs matter here:** INNF 8.3(d) requires evidence of structured planning
and potential for job creation and growth. Assessors weight founder capability
heavily — the funding docs already answer "why are you capable of delivering
it?" at the company level (`docs/funding/innovate-uk-assessment.html`, "Team
Capability" section); these scaffolds turn that into individual, checkable CVs.
