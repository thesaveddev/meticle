Care Management
Digital care records with full audit trails
eMAR & Medication Management
Digital medication rounds with full audit trails. 31-day MAR chart, controlled drug register, missed-dose alerts, and print-ready reports for inspection.
  • 31-Day MAR Chart with audit trail per dose
  • Controlled Drug Register with witness sign-off
  • Missed-Dose Alerts for timely follow-up
  • Stock management with delivery tracking
  • One-click print-ready MAR reports
Daily Care Notes
Record and share daily care observations with shift-based categorisation. Handover-ready and inspection evidence.
  • Shift-based notes (day/night)
  • Category tagging (personal care, behaviour, mood)
  • Author attribution with timestamps
  • Person timeline integration
Person-Centred Support Plans
Build tailored support plans with risk assessments and review dates. Full history retained for CQC inspection.
  • Tailored plan templates by category
  • Risk assessment linked to each plan
  • Review cycle with reminders
  • Active/Archived status management
  • File attachments for supporting docs
Body Mapping & Clinical Records
Visual body maps for pressure areas, wounds, and skin conditions. Clinical scores for health monitoring.
  • Interactive body map with photo evidence
  • Pressure area tracking
  • Clinical scores (BMI, Blood Pressure)
  • Fluid intake monitoring
  • Sleep record tracking

AI-Powered Insights
