Business Plan

Three-year business plan for HMRC review

3.1 Executive Summary

MeticleCare is an AI-first care management platform built for UK CQC-regulated supported-living providers. The platform replaces fragmented paper systems, spreadsheets, and multiple disconnected software tools with a single connected system covering rota planning, medication records (eMAR), daily care notes, compliance evidence, leave management, incidents, training, and family communication.

The platform is deployed in production with 41 backend modules, 484 API endpoints, 78 frontend pages, and 108 database tables. The investment will fund the transition from production-ready platform to validated commercial product with paying customers.

3.3 Product Description

MeticleCare is a multi-tenant SaaS platform with the following core capabilities:

  • Operations: AI-powered rota planner, full eMAR, voice-to-text daily notes, real-time team chat, mobile PWA
  • Compliance: Automated evidence packs (KLOE), CQC readiness scoring, training matrix, competency assessments
  • Intelligence: 5 AI agents (Compliance Officer, Operations Manager, Documentation Specialist, Resident Intelligence, Medication Safety)
  • Finance: Stripe billing, agency management, expense tracking
  • Communication: Team chat, family portal, notifications

  • Full platform: 41 modules in one system (competitors offer 3–5)

Phase 3 (Months 12–18): NHS integration for discharge-to-assess pathways. Trust-level procurement.

5.3 Investor Eligibility (SEIS)

To claim SEIS tax relief, investors must meet the following conditions:

  • UK resident for tax purposes
  • Not employed by the Company (or connected company)
  • Not a director of the Company (unless shares are issued for commercial reasons)
  • Not holding more than 30% of the Company's shares
  • Investment in ordinary shares (not preference shares)

5.4 Tax Relief for Investors

ReliefDetail
Income tax relief50% of investment amount, against income tax liability
Annual investment limit£200,000 per tax year
Capital gains exemptionAny gain on SEIS shares is exempt from CGT if held for 3+ years
Loss reliefIf shares are disposed of at a loss, the loss can be set against income or capital gains
Gift reliefShares can be gifted without triggering CGT
Example: An investor invests £50,000 in MeticleCare under SEIS. They receive £25,000 income tax relief (50%). If the company succeeds and the shares are worth £150,000 after 3 years, the gain is CGT-free. If the company fails and the shares are worth £0, the investor can claim loss relief against income or capital gains.

Risk to Capital Condition

Statement confirming the investment carries genuine risk

6.1 The Condition

HMRC requires that the investment must carry a genuine risk of loss to the investor — there must be no mechanism to guarantee a return of capital or provide a preferential return.

6.2 MeticleCare's Position

No guaranteed return

The shares to be issued are ordinary shares with no preferential dividend, no capital guarantee, and no redemption mechanism. Investors' capital is at risk — if the Company fails, investors may lose part or all of their investment.

No buyback or redemption

The Company will not enter into any agreement to buy back, redeem, or return capital to shareholders outside of a genuine winding-up. There are no side agreements, convertible notes, or other instruments that would provide a preferential return.

No asset-backed security

The shares are not secured against any Company assets. There is no charge, lien, or other security interest in favour of investors.

Commercial rationale

The investment is made on a commercial basis — the Company will use the funds to grow its care management platform and generate revenue from paying customers. The investors' return depends entirely on the Company's commercial success.

No connected party arrangements

There are no arrangements between the Company and the shareholders (other than the subscription agreement) that would reduce the investor's risk below the level expected for an ordinary equity investment.

Confirmation: The investment in MeticleCare Ltd carries a genuine risk of loss to the investor. There are no mechanisms to guarantee a return of capital, provide a preferential return, or otherwise reduce the investor's risk below the level expected for an ordinary equity investment in an early-stage company.
