# Job-creation plan — INNF 8.3(d)

**Status:** internal plan — evidence for the INNF 8.3(d) endorsement file.
Prepared 28 September 2026. **The headcount numbers are not invented:** they
are the headcount plan already in `docs/funding/meticlecare-financial-model.html`
(§4.1), dated August 2026, restated here with roles, indicative salaries, and the
hiring conditions that trigger each hire. Where this document adds a salary
figure the model does not contain, it is marked *indicative* and must be
confirmed by Adetoye before the file is submitted.

---

## 1. Plan summary

MeticleCare's model hires **8 engineers, 4 sales, 3 customer-success, 2 AI/ML,
2 marketing, and 2 ops/admin over three years**, growing the team from 2 founders
to 20 people by Year 3. The hiring plan is demand-triggered: each hire is tied to
a commercial milestone, not to the calendar.

| Year | New hires | Team size (end of year) | Trigger condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 | 8 | First paying customers + funding (see §5) |
| 2 | 6 | 14 | ~96 new customers (financial model §2.1) |
| 3 | 8 | 20 | ~180 new customers (financial model §2.1) |

## 3. Verification and honest status

**What is real today:** 2 founders (both hands-on, both in the plan as the
Year-0 team); a demand-triggered hiring plan dated August 2026 in the financial
model; salary bands marked *indicative*. **What is not real today:** no hires
have been made, no roles advertised, no contracts issued. **The endorsement file
must say so.** An assessor who discovers an unhired "plan" presented as achieved
is worse off than one shown a plan honestly marked as a plan.

## 4. Job quality commitments

- Pay at or above the Real Living Wage for every role.
- Background-check costs (DBS / PVG / AccessNI) covered by the employer.
- Flexible scheduling around care responsibilities — we sell to the care
  workforce; we should be the example.
- Training budget for engineering and customer-success roles from Year 1.

## 5. Funding and dependency chain

Hiring is downstream of the funding ask (£200K–£350K, pitch deck) and the
traction items in the pilot/LOI kit — a hire without its trigger condition is a
guess, and this plan does not guess. See `INNF_8.3d_PILOT_AND_LOI_KIT.md` for
the LOI targets that gate the Year-1 sales and customer-success hires.
